# Superstore Sales Dashboard (2015–2018)

## 📊 Executive Sales Overview
* **Total Revenue:** **$2.26M** generated across **4,922** total orders.
* **Average Sales per Order:** **$230.77**.
* **Seasonality & Trends:** Sales exhibit a clear cyclical pattern—consistently dipping at the start of each year (Q1/February) and surging significantly during the final quarter (Q4).
* **Top Performing City:** **New York City** leads all cities in total revenue generation, followed closely by Los Angeles and Seattle.

## 📈 Top-Performing Categories, Regions & Segments
* **Leading Category:** **Technology** generates the highest total revenue, followed by **Furniture** and **Office Supplies** (which leads in order volume).
* **Customer Segments:** The **Consumer** segment is the primary revenue driver, outperforming Corporate and Home Office segments across all regions.
* **Regional Dominance:** The **West** and **East** regions account for the vast majority of both total sales and order counts.

## ⚠️ Underperforming Areas & Logistics Bottlenecks
* **Regional Lag:** The **South** region records the lowest overall sales volume compared to other territories.
